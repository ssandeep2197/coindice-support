Privacy Policy

Coin & Dice: Fair 3D Rolls · Developer: Sandeep Singh · Effective 5 November 2026

Coin & Dice does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data. It works entirely offline on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software. It does not access the internet.

Information stored on your device

If Save history is turned on (the default), the app keeps a list of your recent coin flips and dice rolls (the results, die type and time) so it can show your history and statistics. This information:

  • is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app;
  • is never sent to the developer or to anyone else;
  • can be deleted at any time in the app under Settings → Clear history, and is removed when you delete the app.

Your app preferences (haptics, felt colour, history on/off) are also stored only on your device.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information. Shake-to-roll uses the standard iOS shake gesture, which does not require permission.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data. It is distributed through Apple's App Store; Apple's own privacy policy applies to App Store downloads and purchases.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
