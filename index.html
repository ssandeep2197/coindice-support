Coin & Dice: Fair 3D Rolls

Support for the iPhone app

Coin & Dice flips a real 3D coin and rolls tabletop dice (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20) using a physics simulation. Results are read from how the coin or dice land.

Frequently asked questions

How do I flip the coin?

On the Coin tab, tap anywhere on the table, flick upward, or press Flip. If the coin ever lands on its edge, it is flipped again automatically.

How do I roll dice?

On the Dice tab, choose a die type (d4 to d20) and how many dice (1 to 6), then shake your iPhone, tap the table, or press Roll. The total and each die's value appear at the top.

Are the results really fair?

Yes. Nothing is decided in advance: each throw starts with random speed and spin, the physics engine simulates the flight and bounces, and the result is read only after everything has stopped moving. The History tab shows how often each face came up, so you can watch the results even out over many rolls.

Shake to roll doesn't respond

Make sure the Dice tab is open and give the phone a short, firm shake. You can always tap the table or press Roll instead.

I don't feel any haptics

Check that Haptics is on in the app's Settings tab, and that System Haptics is enabled in the iPhone Settings app under Sounds & Haptics.

How do I clear my history?

Open the Settings tab and tap Clear history. You can also turn off Save history so new flips and rolls are not stored.

Does the app collect my data?

No. The app works fully offline and collects nothing. See the privacy policy.
